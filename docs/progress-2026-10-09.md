## Open questions in the plan

1. **Imaging path for the archive.** Both work on calculon for this event and
   agree in position (50 % contours coincide): hyperdrive DI + wsclean (fast,
   GPU, lower dynamic range) and P-AIRCARS with self-cal (4.5× dynamic range,
   Slurm/Prefect infrastructure, upstream fixes needed). Suggest P-AIRCARS for
   science images, hyperdrive + wsclean for quick looks and dynamic spectra.
4. **Where production runs.** Calculon works for both paths (GPU cluster for
   hyperdrive/wsclean, `calc-cpu` for P-AIRCARS and analysis). CSCS not tried.
   P-AIRCARS needs a long-running Prefect + Postgres on the login node; check
   whether CSCS allows that before moving.
5. **Self-cal for MWAX data.** Answered for one event: P-AIRCARS self-cal works
   on 2022 MWAX data (4 s / 160 kHz ASVO products).
9. **DI-cal vs P-AIRCARS.** For this event DI calibration alone already gives a
   usable solar image with a good calibrator (PKS0408-65, 3C444); self-cal
   improves dynamic range 4.5×. The earlier Pictor A failure was a
   wrong-grid calibrator, not a method problem.

New questions:

- Per-channel flux scale in P-AIRCARS output jumps by up to ×100 between
  neighbouring channels; top channels (ch173–187) fail. Calibration or
  attenuation scaling per channel needs a look before multi-frequency science.
- Absolute astrometry: background-source checks are hard in solar fields
  (dynamic range); the calibrator field or a Sun-subtracted image is needed.
- Upstream: who submits the P-AIRCARS fixes, and in what order
  (`upstream/slurm-fixes`, `upstream/calibration-fixes`, `upstream/cli-docs`)?
